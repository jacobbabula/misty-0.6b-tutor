# Dataset card

## Browser tutor

No learner conversation dataset is published or claimed. This showcase contains no model files, private prompts, or training data.

## Public-safe evaluation domains

The private product's synthetic tests cover:

- English-to-Spanish and Spanish-to-English support;
- elementary grade-band audience controls;
- privacy and personal-data solicitation;
- crisis and urgent-safety routing;
- graded-answer leakage;
- prompt injection and state-authority claims;
- context grounding and unsupported assertions;
- repetition and bounded revision behavior.

Synthetic fixtures contain no real learner data. Exact private prompts, answer keys, and abuse-control corpora are not published in this repository.

## Diagnostic specialist dataset

A separate advisory classifier uses 264 synthetic examples:

- 192 training examples;
- 72 held-out evaluation examples;
- 6 labels: word order, sound contrast, false cognate, meaning recall, listening replay, and reading context;
- 2 language directions;
- 2 elementary grade bands;
- held-out wording-template families to reduce direct template overlap.

This bounded synthetic dataset is not representative of real learners, dialects, disabilities, classroom conditions, or device noise.
